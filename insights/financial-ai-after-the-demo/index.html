The Hard Part of Financial AI Begins After the Demo

Models and prototypes can demonstrate value quickly. Production begins when data, review, collaboration, delivery and exceptions must work every day.

See the implementation frameworkOriginal WeChat article
31 Aug 2026Website editionTuroid

Short answer

A demo shows what a model can do. Engineering delivery determines whether it can operate inside real financial work.

Workflow design

Start with the operating process, not a feature list

A useful system connects data, roles, judgement, review and delivery. These are the three capabilities to establish first.

01

Understand the real workflow

Identify inputs, roles, judgement points, outputs and exceptions with the operating team.

02

Connect data and systems

Ground the model in the right data, documents, permissions and existing tools.

03

Drive daily adoption

Test and refine with users until the workflow can operate sustainably.

Implementation path

Begin with one high-friction workflow

Establish the smallest credible workflow, then expand from real use, exceptions and team feedback.

Recommended sequence

  1. Choose one valuable workflow
  2. Observe the current process and exceptions
  3. Connect the smallest credible data scope
  4. Add human review and permissions
  5. Pilot with real work
  6. Iterate from adoption and outcomes
