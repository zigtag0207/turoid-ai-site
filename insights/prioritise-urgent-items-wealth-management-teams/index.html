How Wealth Management Teams Can Prioritise Urgent Items

Bring pending discussions, uploads and operational exceptions into one workflow so the team can see what needs attention first.

See the implementation frameworkOriginal WeChat article
31 Aug 2026Website editionTuroid

Short answer

Priority should not depend on who follows up most loudly. A shared queue should show the client, account, issue type, owner, status and supporting context.

Workflow design

Start with the operating process, not a feature list

A useful system connects data, roles, judgement, review and delivery. These are the three capabilities to establish first.

01

One operating queue

Bring pending discussions, uploads and unresolved exceptions into one view.

02

Context stays attached

Connect each item to the relevant client, account, transaction, document or product.

03

Close the loop

Track identification, ownership, discussion, resolution, review and closure.

Implementation path

Begin with one high-friction workflow

Establish the smallest credible workflow, then expand from real use, exceptions and team feedback.

Recommended sequence

  1. Collect open items from every channel
  2. Classify by client, account and issue type
  3. Assign an owner and next action
  4. Keep discussion and files with the item
  5. Review the resolution
  6. Close the item with an audit trail
