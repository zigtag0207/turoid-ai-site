Short answer

Lifecycle management turns a product inventory into an active workflow: what may happen next, what requires attention and who owns the response.

Workflow design

Start with the operating process, not a feature list

A useful system connects data, roles, judgement, review and delivery. These are the three capabilities to establish first.

01

Client and account overview

View products, notionals, status and key dates by client, custodian and account.

02

Early event awareness

Monitor observations, coupons, barriers, autocalls, knock-ins, knock-outs and maturities.

03

Move from lookup to action

Connect upcoming operations, priority watchlists and transaction history.

Implementation path

Begin with one high-friction workflow

Establish the smallest credible workflow, then expand from real use, exceptions and team feedback.

Recommended sequence

  1. Consolidate product and transaction records
  2. Validate material terms and dates
  3. Calculate or import relevant market data
  4. Generate an upcoming-events queue
  5. Assign and record follow-up
  6. Update status through maturity or redemption
