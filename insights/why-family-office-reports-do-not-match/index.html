Why do the same family office assets show different numbers across reports?

Usually, the calculation is not the root problem. The underlying data source, portfolio scope, valuation time, display currency, product status or review process is different.

Short answer

Consistency starts before the report.

A reliable report needs a shared portfolio scope, traceable source data, one display-currency policy and a controlled review-and-release workflow. Without those controls, polished PDFs can still disagree.

Why numbers diverge

Each team may be working from a different version of the portfolio

An RM may use a client-facing workbook, operations may use the latest bank files, and management may receive a PDF prepared on another date. Manual consolidation hides these differences until someone asks why two totals do not match.

Six questions to settle

  • Which custodians, administrators and files are authoritative?
  • Which clients, entities and accounts are included?
  • What valuation date and market-data cut-off apply?
  • Which FX rates and display currency are used?
  • How are structured products and alternatives classified?
  • Who reviews exceptions before the report is released?

A practical operating model

Three capabilities that make reports consistent

01 · Source lineage

Know where every number came from

Connect holdings, transactions, cash flows, product status, market data and FX to their source. When a number changes, the team can identify the input and the processing step.

02 · Reporting basis

Use one portfolio scope and display-currency policy

Retain original currencies while presenting one approved display currency. Apply the same client, entity and account scope across review screens, management views and published packs.

03 · Controlled release

Review the portfolio before exporting

Check positions, allocation, trades, cash flows, structured-product status, performance and currency assumptions. Record exceptions and approvals before the PDF leaves the system.

Shared fact base

Reporting is a cross-team workflow

Relationship managers, operations, investment teams, management and compliance should not reconcile separate spreadsheets at the end of the month. They should review the same portfolio record throughout the process.

Recommended sequence

  1. Register and validate data sources
  2. Review portfolio and account scope
  3. Set display currency and reporting basis
  4. Define the report scope
  5. Resolve exceptions and approve
  6. Export and deliver the report
