Turning Product Terms into Reviewable Due-Diligence Documents

A product due-diligence document is not ordinary generated text. It must connect product terms, risks, suitability considerations and review.

See the implementation frameworkOriginal WeChat article
31 Aug 2026Website editionTuroid

Short answer

A reliable PDD lets the team verify the source terms, inspect identified risks, confirm suitability considerations and record final approval.

Workflow design

Start with the operating process, not a feature list

A useful system connects data, roles, judgement, review and delivery. These are the three capabilities to establish first.

01

Extract product terms

Identify issuer, tenor, underlying assets, return mechanics and trigger conditions from source documents.

02

Structure risk and suitability

Place material risks, client restrictions and judgement points into a consistent framework.

03

Review after generation

Allow product, compliance and client teams to inspect, amend and approve the final document.

Implementation path

Begin with one high-friction workflow

Establish the smallest credible workflow, then expand from real use, exceptions and team feedback.

Recommended sequence

  1. Confirm authoritative product documents
  2. Extract and normalise terms
  3. Check missing or conflicting information
  4. Draft risk and suitability sections
  5. Review and amend with responsible roles
  6. Approve and preserve the delivered version
